## 3. Strategic Rationale & Keyword Placement Guidelines

### A. Search Intent Profile
- **Primary Intent:** All four target pages target high-volume **Informational Search Intent**. Users querying these terms seek authoritative historical explanations, scientific debunking, and educational analyses rather than commercial products or transactional services.
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) Synergy:** Informational queries increasingly trigger Google AI Overviews, Perplexity summaries, and ChatGPT search citations. To capture these positions, every page features at least two question-based H2 headers with immediate direct-answer summaries in paragraph 1.

### B. On-Page Content Integration
1. **Title Tags (`<title>`):**
   - Kept within 50–60 characters to prevent SERP snippet ellipsis truncation.
   - Primary keyword is positioned near the front of the title tag.
2. **Meta Descriptions (`<meta name="description">`):**
   - Kept between 140–160 characters.
   - Accurately summarizes the thesis of the page while naturally integrating the primary keyword and inviting high organic CTR.
3. **Heading Hierarchy (H1 / H2 / H3):**
   - Single distinct `<h1>` tag containing the core topic and primary keyword.
   - Logical `<h2>` sections separating thematic arguments and direct AEO Q&A pairs.
   - Structured `<h3>` subheadings for detailed forensic case examinations.
